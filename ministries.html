WHAT WE DO

Turning Compassion Into Action

Amazing Grace Family Care Foundation focuses on bringing faith, hope and love to vulnerable and less privileged people in society.

Our approach is centred on practical care, including ministering to material needs and reaching people and institutions within the community.

Every outreach is an opportunity to demonstrate compassion and remind people that they are valued and cared for.

PRACTICAL CARE

Meeting Material Needs

AGFC's mission includes ministering to the material needs of vulnerable and less privileged people.

We believe that compassion should not only be expressed through words. Where possible, it should become practical assistance that responds to real needs.

Through community outreach, AGFC seeks to bring encouragement and practical support to the people it reaches.
