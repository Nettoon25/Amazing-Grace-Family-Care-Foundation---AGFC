The AGFC Family, Leadership & Partners

The People Behind The Mission.

Meet the people, leaders, servants and partners who form part of the Amazing Grace Family Care Foundation and contribute to its mission of faith, hope and love.

OUR MINISTRY

People United By Faith & Service

Amazing Grace Family Care Foundation is built around people who share a commitment to serving God and caring for others through faith, compassion and practical service.

From the parent church and founding leadership to ministry workers, associate members and partners, each person contributes in a unique way to the work and vision of AGFC.

This page introduces some of the people and relationships that form part of the AGFC family.

PARENT CHURCH

The Church Behind The Mission

AGFC's ministry is rooted in the faith, values and calling of its parent church and the people who have faithfully served within it.

✦

Amazing Grace Church

Amazing Grace Church is the parent church from which the vision for community care and outreach grew. The church provides spiritual direction, fellowship and a foundation for ministry.

♡

Spiritual Foundation

Through prayer, teaching, worship and fellowship, the church provides a spiritual foundation that shapes the values and direction of the ministry.

◈

Shared Mission

The church and AGFC share a commitment to expressing faith through service, compassion, community care and practical support.

THE FOUNDER

A Vision To Serve Others

Rev. Daniel Ochieng

Rev. Daniel Ochieng is the founding vision bearer of Amazing Grace Family Care Foundation. His desire to see faith expressed through practical care became one of the driving forces behind the establishment of the foundation.

Through years of ministry, teaching and community engagement, he has encouraged people to look beyond themselves and respond to the needs of others with compassion and dignity.

His vision continues to influence the foundation's commitment to faith, hope, love and practical community service.

MINISTRY LEADERSHIP

Serving With Purpose & Humility

The ministry is supported by pastors, ministry workers and volunteers who contribute their gifts and experience to the work of AGFC.

Their responsibilities include spiritual leadership, teaching, prayer, administration, community engagement and coordination of ministry activities.

Together, they seek to serve faithfully while creating opportunities for others to participate in the mission.

PARTNERS & FRIENDS

Working Together For Greater Impact

No ministry grows through the efforts of one person alone. AGFC values relationships with churches, community organizations, institutions, professionals and individuals who share a commitment to serving people.

Our partners and friends may contribute through resources, expertise, referrals, prayer, community connections or participation in specific initiatives.

We are grateful for every relationship that helps extend the reach of the ministry and create meaningful opportunities to serve.
