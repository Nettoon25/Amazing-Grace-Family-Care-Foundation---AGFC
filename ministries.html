OUR MINISTRY

Governance and Management

AGFC General Assembly is the supreme governance body of the organization. It is responsible for the ultimate decision making regarding the organization. The General Assembly elects and delegates the executive board of directors composed of seven members. The board is responsible to provide policy directions and closely monitors AGFC program achievements. AGFC is a registered foundation under the societies of Kenya under section 10 of the society Act.

THE FOUNDER

A Vision To Serve Others

Pastor Gladyce Atemba

Pastor Gladyce Atemba is the founding vision bearer of Amazing Grace Family Care Foundation. Her desire to see faith expressed through practical care became one of the driving forces behind the establishment of the foundation.

Through years of ministry, teaching and community engagement, she has encouraged people to look beyond themselves and respond to the needs of others with compassion and dignity.

Her vision continues to influence the foundation's commitment to faith, hope, love and practical community service.

COMMUNITY

Monitoring and Evaluation Knowledge Management Plan

AGFC will undertake both internal and external evaluation. Post Distribution Monitoring (PDM), will be carried out internally. All activities within the project will be regularly monitored and results against indicators will be collected in monthly reports as well as for each intervention report. A mix of quantitative, qualitative, participatory and observatory means of data collection will be employed for collecting data against key indicators and the findings are incorporated into the reports. Data collection and analysis will done separately for women, men, adolescent boys and girls, disaggregated data throughout the project. The detailed monitoring framework for this project will ensure the community participation throughout the engagement process.
