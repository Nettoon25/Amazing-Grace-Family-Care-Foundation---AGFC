WHAT WE DO

Serving People through Sharing the Word

Amazing Grace Family Care Foundation is committed to sharing the Word of God and bringing faith, hope and love to people within our community and beyond.

Through preaching, teaching, prayer and community outreach, we reach people with the message of God's Word, including vulnerable and less privileged members of society.

Alongside spiritual ministry, we respond to practical needs where possible, seeking to demonstrate God's love through genuine care, compassion and service.

PRACTICAL CARE

Meeting Spiritual Needs

AGFC's mission includes ministering to the material needs of vulnerable and less privileged people.

We believe that compassion should not only be expressed through words. Where possible, it should become practical assistance that responds to real needs.

Through community outreach, AGFC seeks to bring encouragement and practical support to the people it reaches.

PASTORAL MINISTRY

Called To Share The Word

God has entrusted His appointed servants with the responsibility of proclaiming His Word, guiding His people and faithfully carrying out the ministry He has given them.

Faithful To The Calling

The pastors and ministers of Amazing Grace Family Care Foundation are committed to fulfilling the calling entrusted to them by God through preaching, teaching and sharing His Word with His people.

With dedication and humility, they seek to remain faithful to this responsibility, reaching people within the church, the community and those who are vulnerable or less privileged with the message of God's Word, faith, hope and love.
