WELCOME TO AGFC

Faith Put Into Action

Amazing Grace Family Care Foundation (AGFC) is a community-focused organization based in Mradi Embakasi, Nairobi, Kenya.

We bring faith, hope and love to vulnerable and less privileged people in society by ministering to their material needs.

Our work is about caring for people, reaching out to communities and turning compassion into practical action.
