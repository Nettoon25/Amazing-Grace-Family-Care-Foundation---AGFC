WHO WE ARE

Amazing Grace Family Care Foundation

To a high extent there has been numerous effects of the covid-19 pandemic on the general society as people still struggle to transition to the new normal as the covid-19 pandemic dictates, additionally, given the already tough economic time being experienced universally,

Millions of people have been rendered jobless worldwide and families have faced starvation. Most families in Kenya are not exempted from this catastrophe, in fact most of the people facing this kind of suffering are based in the Nairobi cosmopolitan area. Generally this has led to psychological and economic crisis.

Amazing Grace Family Care (AGFC) is committed to the noble cause of bring hope and transforming lives by impacting the amazing grace of God’s love to families through catering for basic need to the widows ,orphans and the elderly and provision of relief aid to communities affected by natural disasters.

WHAT WE DO

Mission Statement

To be God’s preferred channel for the distribution of His love to the world by reaching out to the less privileged (widows, orphans, prisoners, elderly, the vulnerable and the destitute) and to transform their lives by administering the love of God to them through Food Aid Distribution, projects assistance, life skills training, facilitating healthy living conditions and any other way we can assist

Our approach includes ministering to material needs while bringing faith, hope and love to the people we reach.

The foundation's community outreach includes connecting with families and institutions that care for children and other people in need.

FAMILY CARE

Project Justification

The immense suffering that the communities are facing at the current moment worldwide calls for the need to spread the love of God through special care to the needy in the community. The level of vulnerability that existed before the covid-19 pandemic has been heighten as communities struggle for survival, supply of essential items such as food, clothing, shelter and education has become very limited and many families have been stressed economically. The global pandemic covid-19 changed the lifestyle of many families and the global cost of living has intensified an already dire economic situation. Many have been laid off from jobs due to company downsizing of the workers to meet their economic requirements.

COMMUNITY

The Socio-Economic significance of the project

Covid-19 deepened the vulnerability of many people living in the informal settlement. For the past one and a half years to date, many people have struggled to put food on the table, for these reasons there has been increased rate of suicide worldwide as people found it very difficult to deal with the economic crisis as well as the psychological stressors. To prepare communities to deal with future food insecurity and health conditions caused by the pandemic, there is a need to address the immediate need of the community and later on long term adjustment plans can be made to ensure food security.
